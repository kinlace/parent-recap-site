    Where the data goes

    Stays on the Mac

    Everything it reads

    • Raw messages and each night's archive, in ~/ParentRecap
    • Settings in ~/.family, readable only by you
    • The Gmail App Password and the Claude token, in the macOS Keychain. The Wilma CLI keeps its own login in ~/.config/wilmai.
    • WhatsApp's local database is copied, read and the copy deleted. Only the groups you pick are used.
    Leaves the Mac

    Only what the Brief needs

    • To Claude or ChatGPT: tonight's messages from your Sources, on your own plan
    • Through your Gmail: the Brief, to the Recipients you set
    • To Google Calendar, only if you pick it: the new events
    • Pilot families only: an item's text goes to the Parent Recap team when you open its ⭐ or ❌ link
    What it needs

    A Mac and a plan you have

    • A Mac at home that is on or asleep at the time you pick (21:00 unless you change it), with you logged in. It wakes itself. Missed nights catch up, up to a week back.
    • Claude Pro or Max with Claude Code, or ChatGPT Plus or above with Codex
    • One model call a night, plus one for each extra language
    • A setup page in your browser, in Suomi, English or 中文, with more languages to come

        Change something later

        Just say it

        A new school year, a new group, another Recipient or a different language: ask Claude or ChatGPT, or type /parent-recap:manage. It checks the setup and fixes what's broken.
